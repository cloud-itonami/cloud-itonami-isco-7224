# Business Model: Metal Polishing Workshop Scheduling Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7224`
- ISCO-08: `7224`
- Occupation: Metal Polishers, Wheel Grinders and Tool Sharpeners
- Social impact: worker-safety, finish-quality, production-continuity

## Customer

- metal polishing/wheel-grinding/tool-sharpening shops / contractors
- independent metal-finishing crews / crew cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- task/materials-usage/progress-record logging
- abrasive-materials/tooling supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to shop safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a grinding/polishing-execution decision
  (e.g. a specific grinding or polishing operation), ever
- no override of a shop safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (abrasive-wheel hazard, particulate
  exposure, hand/eye injury risk) always route to human sign-off,
  regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
